Security

Your trust is non-negotiable.

We follow the same transparency standards you expect from the $SHIB ecosystem. Here is how we protect you.

Never ask for seed phrases

Legitimate Web3 apps never request your 12/24-word recovery phrase. If any site asks — including ones impersonating $SHIB — it's a scam.

Official contract only

All checks use the verified ERC-20 contract at 0x95aD…C4cE. Always cross-reference on Etherscan.

Open verification logic

Eligibility is verified on-chain: at least 10,000 $SHIB held continuously for 6 months. If your balance ever dropped below 10,000, the hold timer resets.

Red flags to watch for

Beware of DMs promising airdrops, sites asking you to "validate" wallets, or requests to send $SHIB to receive rewards. Real airdrops don't require upfront payments.

Security reminder

This portal only performs read operations until you explicitly sign a claim transaction. Review every transaction in your wallet before approving. When in doubt, verify on shibatoken.com.

FAQ

Common questions

Who is eligible for the loyalty airdrop?

Any wallet holding at least 10,000 $SHIB on Ethereum mainnet, where the earliest on-chain receipt of $SHIB to that wallet was at least 6 months (180 days) ago. Both conditions must be met simultaneously.

Do I need to connect the wallet I've held $SHIB in the longest?

Yes. Verification is wallet-specific. If you've moved tokens between wallets, connect the wallet that currently holds your $SHIB and meets both the balance and hold-time requirements.

Does this work with exchange wallets (Binance, Coinbase)?

No. Exchange custodial wallets cannot be connected via Web3. You must use a self-custody wallet like MetaMask where you control the private keys.

Is there a fee to check eligibility?

Checking eligibility is free — it's a read-only operation. If you claim rewards, standard Ethereum gas fees apply to the claim transaction.

How is the hold period calculated?

We scan all $SHIB transfers to and from your wallet on Ethereum. Your hold period starts when your balance first reaches 10,000 $SHIB and stays above that level continuously. If your balance drops below 10,000 at any point, the timer resets.

Is this an official Shiba Inu program?

This loyalty rewards portal is operated for the ShibArmy community. For official $SHIB announcements, token information, and ecosystem updates, visit shibatoken.com.
