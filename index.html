FAQ

Common questions

Who is eligible for the loyalty airdrop?

Any wallet holding at least 10,000 $SHIB on Ethereum mainnet, where the earliest on-chain receipt of $SHIB to that wallet was at least 6 months (180 days) ago. Both conditions must be met simultaneously.

Do I need to connect the wallet I've held $SHIB in the longest?

Yes. Verification is wallet-specific. If you've moved tokens between wallets, connect the wallet that currently holds your $SHIB and meets both the balance and hold-time requirements.

Does this work with exchange wallets (Binance, Coinbase)?

No. Exchange custodial wallets cannot be connected via Web3. You must use a self-custody wallet like MetaMask where you control the private keys.

Is there a fee to check eligibility?

Checking eligibility is free — it's a read-only operation. If you claim rewards, standard Ethereum gas fees apply to the claim transaction.

How is the hold period calculated?

We scan the official $SHIB contract's Transfer events to find the earliest block where tokens were sent to your address. You must have held $SHIB for at least 6 months (180 days) from that date.

Is this affiliated with the official Shiba Inu team?

This is a community-driven loyalty program inspired by the ShibArmy ethos. For official $SHIB news and resources, always refer to shibatoken.com.
